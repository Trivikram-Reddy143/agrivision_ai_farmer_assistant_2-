- [ ] Wrong OTP is rejected

- [ ] Farmer profile persists after refresh
- [ ] Camera opens on HTTPS/localhost

- [ ] Confidence is shown
- [ ] Scan is saved in history
- [ ] Subsidy entries show official source
- [ ] No secret/token is committed to frontend
